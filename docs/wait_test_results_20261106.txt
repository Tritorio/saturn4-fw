FINDINGS
  - The first wait after the levelling took 11.6 min (the print waited 231 s there, under a weaker rule: probably far too short).
  - Settling time falls with the gap roughly as gap^-1.5 .. -1.7 in layers 1-5 (0.2 -> 0.4 mm: 251 -> 89 s); temperature (cooling) and gap are mixed in run 2.
  - NOISE: gauge noise ~ +-0.36 g (1 sigma) per reading at 5 Hz; the random error of the "change over the window" ~ 0.36 * sqrt(12 / (5 * window s)) g:
    3 s 0.32 g, 5 s 0.25, 10 s 0.18, 20 s 0.12, 30 s 0.10, 60 s 0.07; a sensible limit is >= ~2 x that error.
